Questions patients ask

Emergency? Do not book online. Call 1122 now.

Can you really prescribe online?

Yes, for many common problems. After the video visit the doctor sends a signed e-prescription you can take to any pharmacy.

What can't be prescribed online?

Controlled medicines, such as strong painkillers and sleeping pills, need an in-person visit. So does anything that needs a physical exam.

Do I need to install an app?

No. The video link opens in your phone's browser. If your internet is weak, the doctor can call you instead.

Who sees my details?

Only your doctor. Your notes and prescriptions stay private and are never shared without your consent.
