Questions patients ask first

Can you really prescribe online?

Yes, for many common problems. After the video visit the doctor sends a signed e-prescription you can take to any pharmacy.

Is there anything you will not prescribe online?

Controlled medicines, such as strong painkillers and sleeping pills, need an in-person visit. So does anything that needs a physical examination.

What if it is an emergency?

Do not book a video visit. Call 1122 or go to the nearest emergency department now.

Who sees my details?

Only your doctor. Your notes and prescriptions are kept private and are never shared without your consent.
