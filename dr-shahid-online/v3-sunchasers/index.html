FAQs

Before you book

I live abroad. Can I book for my parents?

Yes. You can book and join the video call from any country, as long as the patient is in Pakistan. Our doctors treat patients who are located in Pakistan.

How much does a visit cost?

An online consultation costs Rs 1,000 with either Dr Muhammad Ahmad Shahid or Dr Khadija Shahid.

Will I get a prescription?

If the doctor decides you need medicine, you receive a signed e-prescription after the visit. Controlled medicines need an in-person visit.

Do I need to install an app?

No. The video link opens in your phone's browser. If your internet is weak, the doctor can call you instead.

Who sees my details?

Only your doctor. Your notes and prescriptions stay private and are never shared without your consent.
